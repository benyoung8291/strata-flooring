Good questions

Things people ask us

Do you supply and install carpet tiles and vinyl planks for strata buildings?

Yes — we're a commercial flooring contractor that handles the full supply and install. We measure your common areas, source carpet tiles and vinyl planks from all major Australian flooring suppliers, install them with minimal disruption to residents, and offer ongoing maintenance programs to protect the investment.

How do I get a quote for strata flooring supply and install?

Use the enquiry form on this page — tell us a bit about your building and what you need. We'll arrange a site visit to measure and assess your common areas, then provide an itemised supply-and-install quote with product specifications and samples — ready to present to your committee or body corporate for approval.

How long does strata flooring replacement take?

Most common area flooring replacements take between 2–5 days depending on the size and scope. We work around building schedules to minimise disruption to residents and tenants. You won't need to shut down the whole building — we stage the work so life goes on.

What flooring is best for strata common areas?

Carpet tiles and vinyl planks are the most popular choices. Carpet tiles are brilliant for lobbies and corridors — they're durable, easy to replace individually, and come in a huge range of styles. Vinyl planks work well in high-traffic and moisture-prone areas. We'll help you figure out what makes sense for your building.

Do you handle body corporate approvals?

We provide detailed proposals with product specifications, samples, and costings that make it straightforward for strata managers and body corporates to approve the work. We know the process and we'll make it as painless as possible.

What does a floor maintenance program include?

Our programs are tailored to your flooring type and include regular deep cleaning, protective treatments, spot repairs, and condition reporting. The goal is simple: extend the life of your floors and keep them looking their best year after year.

Can you match existing flooring in other areas?

Yes — we source from all major Australian flooring suppliers and can match or complement your existing flooring to maintain a consistent look throughout the building. Bring us a sample or a photo and we'll sort it out.

Do you service strata buildings in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane?

Yes — we handle strata flooring projects across all three cities. Our head office is in Melbourne (Thomastown, VIC) and we have teams servicing Sydney and Brisbane metro areas. Whether it's a high-rise in Southbank, an apartment block in Surry Hills, or a unit complex in Fortitude Valley, we've got you covered.

How much does strata flooring replacement cost?

Costs vary depending on the flooring type, area size, and building access requirements. Carpet tiles and vinyl planks are cost-effective options for strata common areas. We provide transparent, no-surprise quotes after a site visit — no hidden costs, no vague estimates. Get in touch for a free quote tailored to your building.

What's the difference between carpet tiles and vinyl planks for strata?

Carpet tiles are ideal for lobbies and corridors — they absorb sound, feel comfortable underfoot, and can be replaced individually if damaged. Vinyl planks are the go-to for wet areas like kitchens and laundries — they're waterproof, scratch-resistant, and come in realistic timber and stone looks. Many strata buildings use a combination of both. Read our detailed comparison guide for more information.
